Tropical Escape

Relax in the tropics with white-sand beaches, overwater villas and island tours.

Overview

Escape to a tropical paradise with turquoise waters, powder-soft beaches and unforgettable sunsets. This package blends relaxation with light adventure — perfect for couples, families or solo travelers looking to unwind.

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival, welcome dinner and beachfront check-in
  • Day 2: Snorkeling tour and island hopping
  • Day 3: Free day at the resort / optional spa
  • Day 4: Sunset cruise and local village visit
  • Day 5: Breakfast and departure

What's Included

  • 4 nights accommodation
  • Daily breakfast
  • Airport transfers
  • Snorkeling tour
  • Sunset cruise
  • 24/7 local support
