Mountain Adventure

Hiking, nature and scenic mountain views for the adventurous traveler.

Overview

Trade the city skyline for mountain peaks. This trip is built for travelers who want fresh air, scenic trails and quiet alpine towns — with comfortable lodges to return to every evening.

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival and lodge check-in
  • Day 2: Guided hike to the scenic ridge trail
  • Day 3: Lake excursion and local village walk
  • Day 4: Cable car ride and summit viewpoint
  • Day 5: Rest day / optional biking
  • Day 6: Waterfall trek
  • Day 7: Breakfast and departure

What's Included

  • 6 nights lodge stay
  • Daily breakfast & dinner
  • Guided hikes
  • Cable car pass
  • Airport transfers
  • 24/7 local support
